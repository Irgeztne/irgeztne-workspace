Principle

Accept the product itself first. Connect it to the system second.

This keeps visual work separate from integration and makes the cause of every defect understandable.

Phase 01

X-ray

Review existing owners, pages, data, and constraints before making changes.

Phase 02

Canon

Build an independent reference and accept its composition, themes, content, and length.

Phase 03

Integration

Connect the accepted reference to one route and one build pipeline.

Phase 04

Verification

Compare preview, browser, export, and actual internal navigation.
