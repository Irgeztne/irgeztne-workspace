FAQ

Answer doubts before the user leaves.

Can users replace the product mockup?

Yes. The demo visual is a placeholder and should be replaced with the user's own screenshot, product image or interface mockup.

Is this for businesses or products?

It is more product-focused than Business / Product: ideal for apps, tools, SaaS, launches and single-product campaigns.

Does it need RSS?

No. Landing pages do not need RSS by default.

Final CTA

Replace this with signup, download, demo, preorder or early access request.
