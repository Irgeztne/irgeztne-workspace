# IRGEZTNE Workspace License History

This file records the public license transition for IRGEZTNE Workspace.

## License timeline

- `v1.0.0-preview.1` to `v1.0.0-preview.3`: MIT License.

## Notes

Earlier preview releases that were already published under the MIT License remain available under the MIT License for copies that were obtained under those releases.
